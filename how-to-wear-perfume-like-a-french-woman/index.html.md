# How to Wear Perfume Like a French Woman for a Long\-Lasting Scent

Knowing **how to wear perfume like a French woman** ups your scent game completely! They say that when it comes to style and beauty, no nation does it better than the French. Of course, the Italians might have something to say about that, but when you look back through history—even way back to classic eras—there has always been something uniquely captivating about the way French women approach their daily beauty rituals. From choosing subtle signature notes to layering scents with [nourishing body oils and lotions](https://beauty.allwomenstalk.com/reasons-to-shop-at-the-body-shop/), their effortless elegance is an art form worth adopting.

They are masters of fashion, masters of food, masters of beauty and, importantly for today’s video topic, masters of perfume! Not only are some of the iconic fragrances in the world created by legendary perfumiers at fashion houses like [Chanel](https://www.chanel.com) and [Dior](https://www.dior.com), but French women also have a knack and expertise in how best to wear a scent in a way that shows it off to the max without overpowering a room. Whether you are exploring [selecting classic fragrance notes](https://perfumes.allwomenstalk.com/best-colognes-to-get-dad-to-have-him-smelling-good-this-fathers-day/) or building your own scent wardrobe, mastering delicate application techniques makes all the difference.

Back in 1997 Kate Winslet implored Leonardo DiCaprio to draw her like one of his French girls, but now it is time to learn how to wear perfume like one of his French girls as well! It is always cool to be armed with some knowledge of how different cultures and nationalities approach universal and everyday beauty habits. You wouldn’t necessarily think that something as simple as applying perfume might be so different between countries, but it’s always cool to make discoveries! Check out this great little video that teaches you exactly **how to wear perfume like a French woman** wears it. You never know, following these steps might just end up with you bagging your very own Leonardo—without the horrendous boat trip, of course!

To master the French art of wearing fragrance, keep these essential rules in mind:

- **Apply to Pulse Points:** Dab lightly on wrists, behind ears, and collarbones where body heat naturally diffuses the scent.

- **Spritz Hair or Scarf:** Spraying a light cloud over hair or a silk scarf allows the fragrance to linger delightfully throughout the day.

- **Never Rub Wrists:** Friction breaks down top notes; let the perfume dry down naturally on your skin.
