# 8 Best Spring Scents & Springtime Perfumes You Need to Try

Spring is finally here, and with the new season comes a fresh batch of marvelous spring scents that you absolutely have to try! There is nothing quite like replacing heavy winter perfumes with light, vibrant floral notes and crisp citrus accords as warm weather arrives. If you want to bottle up the essence of springtime scents and refresh your fragrance collection, take a look at these top 8 best spring scents that smell incredible and wear beautifully all day long.

## 1. Body Tender by Burberry

![Body Tender by Burberry](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/1_body-tender-by-burberry_550x550.jpg)

If you are looking for an aromatic fruity-floral combination, [Burberry](https://www.burberry.com)'s Body Tender is an absolute standout. It is easily one of the ultimate spring perfume scents to add to your collection. This lighter interpretation opens with sparkling notes of lemon, crisp apple, peach, and intriguing absinthe, unfolding into a romantic heart of English rose and fresh tea leaves. Truthfully, it's one of those effortless fragrances you will want to wear every single day.

- **Top Notes:** Lemon, Green Apple, Peach, Absinthe
- **Heart Notes:** Rose, Tea Leaves, Jasmine
- **Base Notes:** Cashmeran, Musk, Amber

## 2. L'Eau Du 34 by Diptyque

![L'Eau Du 34 by Diptyque](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/2_leau-du-34-by-diptyque_336x420.jpg)

If you crave a vibrant, citrus-forward springtime fragrance, [Diptyque](https://www.diptyqueparis.com)'s L'Eau Du 34 is a captivating choice. Bursting with bright notes of bitter orange, lemon, and grapefruit, it balances zestiness with unexpected warm spices like nutmeg, cinnamon, birch leaf, and patchouli. It is light, sweet, and wonderfully complex, making it an instant favorite for warmer days.

## 3. Cherry in the Air by Escada

![Cherry in the Air by Escada](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/3_cherry-in-the-air-by-escada_250x250.jpg)

For those searching for a playful, sweet fruity perfume with an intoxicating sour cherry finish, Escada's Cherry in the Air delivers pure joy. Layering luscious black cherry with juicy raspberry, mandarin, soft marshmallow, and a creamy touch of coconut, it is fun, lighthearted, and undeniably pleasant. If you enjoy bright fruity accords like those in [Burberry Body Tender](#1), this limited-edition blend offers a sweeter, dessert-like twist.

## 4. Feerie Spring Blossom by Van Cleef & Arpels

![Feerie Spring Blossom by Van Cleef & Arpels](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/4_feerie-spring-blossom-by-van-cleef-arpels_336x420.jpg)

Beyond the enchanting jewel-like bottle featuring a fairy perched atop the cap, Van Cleef & Arpels Feerie Spring Blossom contains a fragrance that is equally whimsical. Combining succulent cherry blossom notes with delicate magnolia and velvety musk, it strikes a harmonious balance between elegant florals and fresh fruits that appeals across every age group.

| Fragrance | Primary Notes | Scent Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body Tender | Lemon, Rose, Apple, Absinthe | Fresh Floral & Fruity |
| L'Eau Du 34 | Bitter Orange, Patchouli, Nutmeg | Spicy Citrus |
| Cherry in the Air | Black Cherry, Marshmallow, Coconut | Sweet Gourmand Fruity |
| Feerie Spring Blossom | Cherry Blossom, Magnolia, Musk | Soft Floral |

## 5. Daisy Eau so Fresh Sunshine by Marc Jacobs

![Daisy Eau so Fresh Sunshine by Marc Jacobs](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/5_daisy-eau-so-fresh-sunshine-by-marc-jacobs_550x506.jpg)

No list of the best spring scents would be complete without a signature creation from [Marc Jacobs](https://www.marcjacobs.com). Daisy Eau so Fresh Sunshine is a bright, cheerful bouquet packed with apple blossom, ripe strawberry, pink grapefruit, and day lily, grounded by white oakmoss and soft amber wood. Much like the sunny citrus notes found in [Diptyque's L'Eau Du 34](#2), this uplifting fragrance practically begs to be worn on warm, sunny afternoons.

## 6. Eau Legere by Bottega Veneta

![Eau Legere by Bottega Veneta](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/6_eau-legere-by-bottega-veneta_550x447.jpg)

For a more understated, sophisticated take on springtime scents, Bottega Veneta's Eau Legere offers a refined alternative to bright florals. Blending subtle pink pepper with gardenia, jasmine, luminous leather, juicy plum, and earthy oakmoss, it delivers a softer, smoother fragrance experience that lingers beautifully on the skin.

- **Key Accords:** White Floral, Leather, Fresh Spicy, Fruity
- **Best For:** Evening wear and refined springtime events

## 7. Happy in Bloom by Clinique

![Happy in Bloom by Clinique](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/7_happy-in-bloom-by-clinique_550x432.jpg)

[Clinique](https://www.clinique.com) is famous for creating joyful, uplifting fragrances, and Happy in Bloom is no exception. Bursting with fresh green notes, crisp yellow plum, lily of the valley, and white wood, this bright floral perfume creates an instant mood boost. If you love the cheerful energy of [Marc Jacobs Daisy Sunshine](#5), this clean, sweet scent is a must-try for your daily routine.

## 8. See by Chloé

![See by Chloé](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/perfumes/2013/04/8_see-by-chlo_550x497.jpg)

To complete your seasonal scent wardrobe, See by Chloé offers a chic, graceful option that balances floral warmth with modern sophistication. Featuring elegant apple blossom, sweet jasmine, ylang-ylang, soft vanilla, and cozy musk, it is a versatile spring perfume scent that seamlessly transitions from morning brunch to evening dinner dates.
