# 17 Best Perfumes to Wear to School for a Fresh Everyday Scent

When picking the best perfumes to wear to school, balance is everything. You don’t want a heavy, cloying perfume that turns into a pervasive miasma in a crowded lecture hall or classroom. Instead, you want a light, delicious back to school fragrance that keeps you feeling confident and helps you smell good all day at school. Your signature everyday scent should express your personal style while remaining fresh, inviting, and subtle. Whether you're heading to high school classes or college lectures, here are the top school fragrances to try:

- Opt for fresh, airy Eau de Toilette or body mist formulas for daily wear.

- Apply to pulse points right after moisturizing for longer lasting scent.

- Keep application light so your fragrance remains subtle and refreshing.

## 2. Princess – Vera Wang

Wear this if you are independent and playful. It has a flirty and adventurous vibe with overtones of the sweetest cupcake in the bakery. Sparkling dark chocolate and guava notes mix with delicate florals, making it one of the best perfumes for school when you want something fun and sweet without being overwhelming.

## 3. Infinite Pleasure – Just Girl – Estelle Vendome

A great in-between scent, fruity enough for a fun day at school but still with a few musky undertones that show you are more of an adult than a child. It balances youthful energy with refined elegance, making it ideal if you prefer a subtle, lingering trail throughout your busy campus day.

## 4. Live Colorfully – Kate Spade

Citrus and florals open this sweet scent, but a lingering hint of musk stays with you long after the top notes have dissipated. With hints of water lily, mandarin, and star anise, it offers a bright boost for early morning classes. If you like refreshing floral notes similar to [Jo Malone Peony and Blush Suede](#14), this bright fragrance is a wonderful addition to your routine.
